Why Volunteer

Make a Meaningful Impact

Legal Chords is committed to promoting legal literacy, empowering individuals with knowledge of their rights, and encouraging ethical leadership through education, advocacy, community engagement, and collaborative initiatives.

Empower Through Education

Help simplify complex legal concepts and make legal knowledge accessible to young people and communities.

Build Leadership Skills

Develop your leadership, teamwork, communication, and project management skills in a supportive environment.

Drive Social Change

Contribute to creating a society where people understand their rights, fulfill responsibilities, and make informed decisions.

What to Expect

Our Selection Process

Step 1

Application Review

We review all applications carefully to understand your background, interests, skills, and motivation for volunteering with Legal Chords.

Step 2

Interview

Shortlisted applicants may be invited for an interview (virtual or physical) to discuss their application and fit for the team.

Step 3

Orientation & Probation

Successful applicants undergo orientation and may have a probationary period to ensure a good fit for both parties.
